Every shaded cell is a figure taken from that company's own earnings release on SEC EDGAR — hover for the filing behind it, click to open the document. Where a company publishes the growth rate itself (constant currency, organic, comparable sales) that figure is used; otherwise growth is computed from the current and prior-year levels in the same filing. Nothing is extrapolated, and a cell that cannot be read stays blank. Fiscal quarters are assigned to the calendar quarter whose end date is nearest, so Walmart's February–April quarter reads as Q1. Estimate columns carry only numeric guidance a company published on the same metric. Colour diverges from zero growth; the credit band runs on its own scale, inverted, and is excluded from the Average and Median rows.
