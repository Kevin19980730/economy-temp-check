Method

Every figure comes from the company's own earnings release filed with the SEC. Where a company publishes the growth rate itself — constant currency, organic, or comparable sales — that published figure is used, because it carries adjustments that cannot be reconstructed from reported levels. Where it does not, growth is computed from the current and prior-year levels in the same filing. The rule column shows which applied.

Conventions

Calendar alignment. A fiscal quarter is assigned to the calendar quarter whose end date is nearest, and collisions are broken by forcing the sequence to advance one quarter at a time. That puts Walmart's February–April quarter in Q1 and keeps Costco's twelve-week quarters, which end mid-calendar-quarter, one-to-one and stable year over year.

Estimates. The estimate columns are filled only from numeric guidance a company published itself on the same metric, shown at the midpoint of the range. Qualitative outlooks are left blank rather than converted.

Credit rows. The credit band carries charge-off and delinquency rates, not growth. They run on their own colour scale, inverted so a rising rate reads as bad, and are excluded from the Average and Median rows.
